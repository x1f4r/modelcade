# Modelcade 2.0.0

Rebuilt for ChatGPT's new model list, Power slider, and speed tiers.

- Applies choices through ChatGPT's own picker callbacks instead of driving hidden menus, so the native menu never opens, flickers, or leaks.
- Builds every choice from what ChatGPT offers your account at that moment: GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, Sol and Luna, GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra and Luna, GPT-5.5, Default mode, Instant through Pro in Chat, Light through Max and Ultra in Work, and Standard, Fast and Ultrafast speed.
- Commits each change the way ChatGPT's menu does, so it survives reloads.
- New picker design with a model wheel, a magnetic power rail, per-model palettes (adding Astra), and special stops for Instant, Max, Ultra and Pro.
- New sound engine: musical, stereo-panned detents, model clicks, speed whoosh, Max power-up, Pro chime, and the Ultra lever and jackpot (arcade or slot machine), with a generated room reverb.
- Opens above the composer when there isn't room below.
- Settings move to the toolbar popup: on/off, sounds and volume, celebrations, jackpot style.
- Alt-click the composer control for ChatGPT's own picker at any time.
- Stops reading account and workspace metadata, and deletes the 1.x per-account Ultra preference on upgrade.
